01
The pain

You have never been able to say: “you may spend this much, and I can stop you instantly.”

Hand over a card number and you hand over everything the card can do. Give an AI assistant your details and you are trusting an app’s promise, not a rule. Every payment instrument we have binds authority to a credential — so whoever you cut off simply comes back with a new one.

224,603authorised push payment fraud cases in the UK in one year — money moved with the customer’s own valid authority. Source: UK Finance / PSR.
835unique humans have registered 1,185 agent wallets in World’s AgentBook. Verified by us on-chain. Every product built on it gates an API endpoint. None gates money.
02
The solution

Bind the authority to the human behind the machine.

World already answers “is there one unique person behind this wallet?” — the AgentBook registry maps an agent address to an anonymous human identifier, on-chain. We read that registry from inside a payment contract and enforce every rule against the person:

The budget is theirsTwo of their agents share one rolling 24-hour cap. A new address is not a new allowance.
The revocation is theirsCut them off and every agent they will ever operate loses access in the same transaction.
Widening costs a faceSpending inside the cap needs nobody present. Raising it, or moving the payee, needs a fresh Selfie Check.
03
The product

Five taps, then you stop thinking about it.

1 · Prove you’re one personSelfie Check inside World App. No document leaves the phone.
2 · Make a cardName it, set a daily limit and a per-payment limit, choose exactly who it can pay.
3 · Hand it to your assistantPaste the person ID of whoever will spend. Nothing else is needed — not their address.
4 · Watch it workA plain-language feed: what was spent, when, to whom, how much of today’s budget is left.
5 · Stop it, any timeOne tap kills the person, not the key. New addresses included.
Need more room?Raising the limit asks for your face. That is the only moment it does.
